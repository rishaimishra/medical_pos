Big Picture Architecture

How All 5 Panels Work Together (Zero Overlap, Complete Security)

Every type of person gets their own login screen and private area. A patient can never enter the billing counter, a store owner can never open your CEO revenue dashboard, and Pharmacy A can never see Pharmacy B's medicines or customers.

1. Public & Setup/setup/wizard

New Store Self-Onboarding

A brand-new medical store owner opens the website, fills a 4-step form (Shop Name, Drug License, GSTIN, Owner Login), and within 60 seconds gets their own private pharmacy workspace + an automatic 14-Day Free Growth Trial.

2. Pharmacy Workplace/admin/*

Store Admin & Staff Panel

Where daily business happens: scanning barcodes, billing patients, managing multiple branches, checking expiring strips, paying wholesalers, filing GSTR-1/3B, closing the evening cash drawer, and paying SaaS subscription via Razorpay.

3. SaaS Owner Tower/super-admin/*

Platform CEO Control Tower

Your headquarters. Create pricing plans synced to Razorpay, track Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), see stores running out of quotas (upsell leads), spot inactive stores (churn alerts), suspend non-payers, or inspect any store safely.

4. Patient App/customer/*

Customer / Patient Portal

Patients sign in with their email/phone to download old pharmacy bills across multiple stores they visited, upload prescription photos for pickup/delivery, chat with the pharmacist, track chronic monthly medicine refills, and request returns.

5. Vendor Portal/supplier/*

Wholesale Supplier Extranet

Distributors log in to view Purchase Orders sent by the pharmacy, quote updated rates, enter challan/invoice numbers when dispatching trucks, view stock levels of batches they supplied, and message the purchasing manager.

Scenario A — N-Store Customer Bill Aggregation (LIVE • Module 3.1.1):
Suppose Ramesh buys diabetes strips at Apex Pharmacy (Store 1) without registering on the portal—he just gives his mobile number 9876543210 at the counter. Next week he buys antibiotics at City Care Chemists (Store 2) using the same phone number. Each store saves its own isolated customer record (uk_store_customer_phone = [storeId, phone]), so neither store can ever see the other store's list or credit dues! Later, when Ramesh registers & verifies once on /customer/login with 9876543210, the system links all matching phone profiles (getLinkedCustomerIds) so Ramesh sees ALL N stores' bills (Store 1, Store 2, ..., Store N) in one personal portal—even new purchases from a 4th store post-login—each with the originating Store Name + Branch badge and downloadable Tax Invoice!

Scenario B — Private Multi-Store & Branch Prescription Routing (LIVE • Module 3.10.1):
When uploading a doctor's prescription photo or sending support chat, patients never broadcast it to unrelated pharmacies. Both Prescriptions (Tab 3) and Messages (Tab 5) provide a Target Pharmacy Store & Target Branch Selector (saving exact PatientMessage.storeId + branchId and emailing ONLY that chosen pharmacy). Strict server-side isolation blocks Store B with HTTP 403 / 404 on Store A's Rx files—and even blocks Store A's Main-branch staff from opening prescriptions routed specifically to Store A's Annex branch!

How Razorpay Works Inside This System (Step-by-Step + Real Numbers)

Think of Razorpay as our automated bank collector. Here is every moving part explained in simple English so anyone in sales, operations, or management can understand how money moves from pharmacies to your bank account safely.

Real Money Math #1: What is "Proration Credit"?

When a pharmacy upgrades mid-month, we never punish them by making them pay twice for unused days! Our backend calculates remaining-day credit and subtracts it before adding 18% GST.

Real Example (Verified by Live Test Suite!):
• Pharmacy bought Monthly Starter (₹799) on Day 1.
• Shortly after, they upgrade to Yearly Scale (₹12,000).
• Unused credit from Monthly Starter = ₹799.
• Net Taxable Amount = ₹12,000 - ₹799 = ₹11,201.
• Plus 18% GST on ₹11,201 → Final Razorpay Order total.
• Once paid, old Monthly Starter sub becomes expired so CEO MRR counts only the new plan!

Real Concept #2: Why "Immutable Plan Versioning" & "Reconcile All"?

Razorpay has a strict banking rule: once a Plan ID (say plan_Old123 at ₹1,999/mo) exists on Razorpay, nobody can change its price or billing cycle because existing subscribers already signed mandates for ₹1,999.

  • If you change Limits/Features (e.g., increase Growth Staff limit from 15 → 20): Updates locally in 10ms, keeps the same Razorpay plan_id!
  • If you change Price or Cycle (e.g., ₹1,999 → ₹2,299): Automatically creates a brand-new Razorpay Plan ID (plan_New456) for new buyers while existing buyers keep renewing peacefully on their grandfathered rate!
  • "Reconcile All with Razorpay": Pulls live plans from Razorpay API and compares every local plan's amount/interval against Razorpay, auto-fixing any mismatch in 1 click.

Real Concept #3: Webhook Replay Protection ("Idempotency")

Suppose a bank server is slow, so Razorpay sends the subscription.charged webhook alert 3 times for the exact same ₹1,999 payment. If a normal system isn't protected, the customer would get 3 invoices and 3 months added instead of 1!

How We Prevent That: Every Razorpay alert carries a unique ID (x-razorpay-event-id). 1) First, we verify the digital signature using RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET (forged requests get 401).
2) Next, we check our RazorpayWebhookEvent table: on Alert #1 we process payment & mark processed = true. On Alerts #2 & #3, our server responds 200 OK (duplicate ignored) with zero double-billing!

Real Concept #4: 7-Day Grace Period vs Hard Lockout

Imagine 15 sick patients waiting in line at a medical shop and the owner's bank card failed at midnight! Shutting down the billing counter immediately would be disastrous.

  • Days 1–7 after Failure ("Grace Period"): Store sees an amber warning banner + gets an email to update card at /admin/billing. Counter POS sales stay 100% open so patients never wait, while non-core BI exports pause until renewed.
  • After 7 Days ("Hard Suspended"): New POS bills & staff edits stop (HTTP 402), EXCEPT /admin/billing (so they can pay right away!) and Compliance/GST/Audit exports (legal records never held hostage!).

Complex Words Made 100% Simple

Jargon Buster: Every Technical & Business Term Explained With Examples

Use these plain-English explanations when explaining the system to pharmacy owners, hospital buyers, investors, or non-technical teammates.

1. What does "Bidirectional" mean here?

"Bidirectional" simply means two-way street—information flows both ways instead of just one person shouting and getting no reply. Where does it happen in our app?

  • Patient ↔ Store Chat: Customer uploads a prescription/question (direction: in) and Pharmacist replies directly back inside the same thread (direction: out).
  • Supplier ↔ Store PO: Pharmacy sends Purchase Order; Supplier logs into Supplier Portal and quotes updated prices/dispatch tracking; Admin confirms or messages back.
  • Store Profile ↔ MAIN Branch: Updating store DL/GSTIN in Admin Settings updates the primary MAIN branch automatically and vice versa.
  • Local Plans ↔ Razorpay: Creating a plan pushes it to Razorpay; "Reconcile All" pulls Razorpay plans back to confirm parity!

2. Multi-Tenant Isolation (storeId vs branchId)

Imagine a huge apartment building. The software is the building itself. Each independent pharmacy buyer gets their own locked apartment (storeId). Nobody can walk into another buyer's apartment. Inside one pharmacy's apartment, they can have multiple rooms like Main Shop + Hospital Counter (branchId), and their owner can walk between their own rooms using the Branch Switcher!

3. FEFO (First Expiry, First Out)

Groceries use FIFO (sell oldest arrival first). Pharmacies use FEFO (sell whichever batch expires soonest first).

Example: You have Batch A (expires Dec 2026) and Batch B (expires Mar 2027) of Dolo-650. At checkout, POS auto-picks Batch A first so it sells before expiring—and if Batch X expired yesterday, POS locks it red so no staff can ever accidentally sell an expired pill!

4. ACID Transaction & Rollback ("All-or-Nothing Safety")

Every bill touches 5 places at once: deducts batch stock, writes sale row, saves payment, updates customer credit, writes audit log. Wrapping it in an ACID Transaction means if power/internet cuts on step 4, everything rolls back cleanly—no stock gets wrongly deducted without a saved invoice!

5. Weighted Average Cost (in GRN Receiving)

When a wholesaler sends new stock of the same medicine + same batch at a slightly different wholesale price, our GRN desk merges the quantities and computes the true average cost automatically.

Example: Already had 10 strips bought at ₹80. Today received 10 more same-batch strips at ₹90. System merges to 20 strips with weighted cost = ₹85/strip, keeping your Profit & Loss accurate!

6. COGS Profit vs Simple Sales Total

Old software subtracts "total bills paid to suppliers this month" from sales—which is wrong if you still hold unsold stock on shelves. We use COGS (Cost of Goods Sold): every sold strip looks up its exact batch purchase cost, giving the true real-time profit margin per invoice!

7. Discount-Proportional Refund

Prevents return fraud or over-refunding. Suppose a patient bought a ₹100 syrup with a 10% bill discount (paid ₹90). When they return that syrup later, the software refunds ₹90 (what they actually paid), not full ₹100!

8. Feature Gating (HTTP 402), Quotas & X-Quota-Warning

Both the frontend sidebar (Lock + PRO badges) AND backend APIs check the store's active plan. When a Starter store reaches 80%+ of monthly bills, our API sends a gentle X-Quota-Warning alert rather than crashing silently mid-queue—nudging the owner to upgrade smoothly!

9. Schedule H / H1 / Narcotics Compliance

Indian Drug Control law requires strict patient + prescribing doctor registers for habit-forming or high-alert antibiotics (Schedule H / H1). Our system flags those medicines at POS and generates a 1-click Drug Inspector audit report.

10. Z-Report (Day-End Cash Drawer Closing)

At 10 PM shop closing, the cashier physically counts notes in the cash box (₹500 × 12, ₹200 × 5) and types the counts into Financials → Cash Drawer. The system compares physical cash vs recorded Cash Sales minus Cash Returns/Expenses and exposes any ₹200 shortage instantly.

11. SaaS CEO Metrics (MRR, ARR, ARPU, GMV, Churn)

  • MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue): Normalized monthly subscription income. A ₹12,000 yearly plan counts as ₹12,000 ÷ 12 = ₹1,000 MRR!
  • ARR (Annual Run Rate): MRR × 12 (e.g., ₹1,000 MRR = ₹12,000 ARR).
  • ARPU: Average Revenue Per Paid Store.
  • GMV (Gross Merchandise Value): Total retail medicines billed by all pharmacies using your software (great bragging stat for investors: e.g., "₹5 Crore+ pharmacy bills processed!").
  • Logo Churn %: % of paid pharmacies that cancelled in the last 30 days.

12. Other Helpful Terms in Brief

  • SAC 998314: Official Indian GST Service Accounting Code printed on SaaS software tax invoices (SUB-INV-YYYY-00001) so pharmacies can claim 18% GST Input Credit.
  • RBAC & Manager Override: Role rules (Cashiers cannot see wholesale cost or give >10% discount without Manager PIN approval).
  • UTF-8 BOM & OpenXML .xlsx: Ensures Excel shows the ₹ Rupee symbol cleanly without garbled text and blocks formula-injection hacks.
  • Append-Only Audit Trail: Security log blocking Edit/Delete (405) and auto-masking passwords as [REDACTED].

13. GST-Inclusive MRP Tax Extraction (Indian Pharma Law)

Unlike restaurants that add GST on top of menu prices, Indian medicine MRPs already include GST! Our POS extracts the GST portion backwards automatically—and computes inclusive totals before subtracting discounts so tax never miscalculates.

Example: Medicine MRP = ₹112 (GST = 12%). Base Taxable Value = 112 ÷ 1.12 = ₹100. GST Tax Split = ₹6 CGST + ₹6 SGST. Patient pays exact ₹112!

14. Audited Support View ("Impersonation") & UX Locking

  • Support Impersonation Banner: When the CEO clicks "Support View" to troubleshoot a pharmacy's problem, they get a 30-minute window with a bright red top bar ("CEO Support Session — Audit Logged") so the store owner sees complete transparency.
  • <FeatureGate /> + Downgrade Warnings: Locked features show a sleek Lock + PRO badge opening <UpgradeRequiredModal />—and if a Growth store with 3 active branches tries downgrading to a 1-branch Starter plan, /admin/billing warns them upfront!
